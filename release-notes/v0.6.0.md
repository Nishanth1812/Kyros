Kyros 0.6.0 · Planning tools and sync readiness

- **Safer category management.** Rename or archive custom categories, and delete a category by reassigning its transactions and recurring schedules. Removed category budgets are recorded for later sync.
- **Recurring transactions.** Create daily, weekly, monthly, or yearly schedules, review due items on the Dashboard, and apply them to transaction history.
- **Savings goals and budget alerts.** Track goal contributions and deadlines, see projected monthly savings, and get a warning at 80% of a monthly budget and an over-limit alert at 100%.
- **System, light, and dark themes.** Choose an appearance in the Data tab or follow the device setting. The selection is saved on the device.
- **History search and tags.** Search descriptions, category names, and amounts, or filter by hashtags such as `#travel`.
- **Backup V2.** Export goals, recurring schedules, category state, timestamps, and deletion markers. Existing V1 backups remain restorable with safe defaults.
- **Local sync readiness.** SQLite can export and apply timestamped deltas with last-write-wins conflict handling and deletion markers. No network sync service is included; financial data stays on the device.

Version code: `9`. Android upgrades require the same signing key.
