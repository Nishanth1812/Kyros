Kyros 0.2.0 · transaction management and spending overview

- **Categories.** Pick built-in or custom income and expense categories when adding a transaction.
- **Edit and delete.** Correct any entry in place; deleting asks for confirmation and never removes its category.
- **History filters.** Narrow the newest-first list by type, category, and an inclusive date range.
- **Dashboard.** See lifetime balance, this month's income and expense totals, and this month's expenses by category — all derived from your saved entries.
- **Safe upgrade.** Existing 0.1 entries are migrated in place with their amounts unchanged and assigned to the matching type's `Other` category, ready to recategorize.
